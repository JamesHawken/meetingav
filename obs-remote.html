    Practice

    Try the switching without JW Library or a second screen: pretend something is showing and watch the camera cut, then go back. The real JW Library display is ignored until you stop.

    Drag the lines on the picture to fit your platform: the two dotted edges between the areas, the bottom edge up to ignore the audience, and the top of the table area down for the reader.

    For the Reader shot, put the line between a seated head and a standing one at the table. Picking a shot yourself turns following off until you turn it back on. JW Library media always takes priority.

    Turn parts of the shot that’s on air on or off.

    OBS takes the sound desk's feed, levels it (quiet voices up, loud ones and songs held back, never clipping) and plays it into VB-CABLE. In Zoom, set the microphone to CABLE Output and turn off Zoom's automatic volume. The hall speakers aren't affected.

    A shot you pick yourself always cuts straight away.

    When Meeting AV starts (and on “Get ready again”), it opens JW Library and joins this Zoom meeting. The invite link, with its passcode, or just the meeting ID. Leave it empty to open Zoom yourself.

    For a new or backup PC: the meeting times, shots, following people, sound and timer choices go in one file. Load it in Meeting AV on the other PC. The OBS scenes move separately: in OBS, Scene Collection → Export, then Import on the other PC.

    For the countdown, Meeting AV switches JW Library’s second display off, and back on as it reaches 0:00, leaving JW Library on the meeting’s media. Pictures and videos reach the TV again once it’s back; JW Library on the TV by the timer gives it back sooner.

    Pick a screen the speakers can see. It fills that screen: green while there's time, amber in the last minute, red and counting up once over time. With no part loaded it shows the time of day. Meeting AV puts it back on the same screen next time it starts.
